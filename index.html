Privacy Policy

Hush — Sleep & Focus Sounds

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Hush collects no data. None.

Hush collects no personal data, has no accounts, and sends nothing off your device. Every sound layer, mix, and preset you build stays entirely on your phone. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers.

What's stored, and where

Hush stores your mix settings — which sounds are layered, their volumes, the tone-tilt position, and any named presets you save — in this app's local storage on your device only. None of it is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Network

The core app works fully offline and does not request internet access for playback, mixing, presets, or the sleep timer. The only time anything leaves the app is if you choose to make a one-time Supporter Pack purchase (handled entirely by the Google Play Store, subject to Google's own privacy policy — we never see your payment details) or, where offered, tap an optional link to an external support page, which opens in your browser (subject to that page's own privacy policy, and clearly disclosed as external before it opens).

Permissions, in plain terms

  • Foreground service / media playback — so your sound mix keeps playing in the background with the screen off, with lock-screen/notification playback controls.
  • Notifications — to show the ongoing-playback notification and its controls.
  • Boot receiver — only if you enable "Resume last mix automatically" in Settings (off by default); re-starts your last saved mix after a restart. No data leaves the device to do this.

Hush does not request microphone, camera, location, or contacts access.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.
