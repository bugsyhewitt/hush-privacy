Privacy Policy

Hush — Sleep & Focus Sounds

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Hush collects no data. None.

Hush collects no personal data, has no accounts, and sends nothing off your device. Every sound layer, mix, and preset you build stays entirely on your phone. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers.

What's stored, and where

Hush stores the named presets you save (which sounds, their volumes and the tone-tilt position) and your settings in this app's local storage on your device only. None of it is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Network

The core app works fully offline and does not request internet access for playback, mixing, presets, or the sleep timer. Every sound and feature is free. If you choose to leave an optional tip (Settings → Donate), Google Play handles the purchase under Google's own privacy policy; we never see your payment details, and the app keeps no record of the payment. A tip unlocks nothing, and the app never asks.

Google's billing library, which the tip jar uses, includes a diagnostics component that would send anonymous billing diagnostics to Google. Hush has no internet permission, so Android blocks it and nothing is sent. While a tip you started is still being finished, Hush keeps a small marker on your device so it can complete the tip the next time you open it; it never records what you paid.

Permissions, in plain terms

  • Foreground service / media playback — so your sound mix keeps playing in the background with the screen off, with lock-screen/notification playback controls.
  • Notifications — to show the ongoing-playback notification and its controls.
  • Boot receiver — only if you turn on "Offer my last preset after a restart" in Settings (off by default); after a restart it shows a notification offering the last preset you played or saved. Nothing plays until you tap it, and no data leaves the device.
  • Google Play billing (and view network connections, which Google's billing library adds) — only to take an optional tip, if you choose to leave one. Google Play handles the payment; the app receives no payment details.

Hush does not request microphone, camera, location, or contacts access.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.
